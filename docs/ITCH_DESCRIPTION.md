# SLINGSHIFT

**A little thrust. A lot of gravity. Find your line.**

Pull back. Read the orbit. Let go.

Guide a tiny spacecraft through twelve carefully arranged corners of space. Curve around glowing worlds, slip between twin suns, and thread a path through the rocks. Your dotted trajectory tells the truth. What you do with it is up to you.

Missed the gate? Retry instantly. Almost there? You have one emergency burn.

- Twelve orbital puzzles, with thirty-six stars to chase.
- A live trajectory preview and optional gravity/route overlays.
- One limited midflight correction, with slow time while aiming.
- Procedural planets, quiet ambient sound and responsive mouse/touch controls.
- Local saved progress. No accounts or connection required after loading.

**Controls:** Drag backward from the ship to launch. Drag empty space to refine your aim, then press Launch. During flight, drag anywhere for one correction. R retries; Escape pauses. Keyboard controls and adjustable audio/effects are available in the game.

A small game about finding a way through.
